Giftara

Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Giftara is a gift concierge app made by Ron Brand (RBrand Customs LLC) in New Jersey. This page explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what stays on your phone.

In short: Giftara has no accounts and no ads, and it doesn't track you. The people you keep in The Book, your conversations and your saved gifts are stored on your phone. When you ask the concierge something, your message goes through Giftara's server to Google's Gemini to write the reply, and Giftara's server doesn't keep it.

What stays on your phone

  • The Book: the people you buy for and what you've noted about them (tastes, sizes, dates, budgets, gifts given).
  • Your conversations with the concierge, your reserved gifts, the cards you write and your settings.

This is saved on your phone, not encrypted, and not sent to us. It stays until you delete it, clear your conversations in Settings, or uninstall the app. A backup you make in Settings is a file you save wherever you choose; it isn't sent to us.

When you talk to the concierge

To write each reply, Giftara sends your message, the earlier messages in that conversation, and what The Book holds about the person you're asking about through Giftara's server to Google's Gemini service. The same happens when you ask the app to write a card.

  • Giftara's server (run on Cloudflare) passes the request to Google and the reply back. It doesn't store your messages.
  • Google uses the request to produce the reply. Under the terms for Google's paid Gemini service, Google doesn't use it to improve its products, and keeps it only for a limited time to check for misuse. See Google's Gemini API terms.

Please don't share anything in a conversation you wouldn't want sent this way, such as passwords or financial details.

A random install ID

When you first open Giftara, it makes a random ID for that install. It isn't tied to your name, email or phone number. The app sends it with each request so the server can count how many concierge chats that install has used each month and whether it has Premium. The server keeps only those counts and the Premium status for that ID.

Gift photos from eBay

To show a photo of a suggested gift, Giftara's server asks eBay for listings using only the gift's maker and name. The photo then loads directly from eBay, which, like any website, sees your device's internet address. Tapping a photo opens its eBay listing. Photos are never sent to Google's Gemini.

Premium purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Giftara never sees your card or bank details. Giftara uses RevenueCat to check and manage the subscription: RevenueCat receives your install ID and your purchase records from Google Play. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Shops and links

"Where to buy" and shop links open other websites, such as a store or a search page. What you do there is covered by those sites' own policies. A link you share into Giftara from a shop is kept on your phone.

Reminders

Occasion reminders are scheduled on your phone by the phone itself. No reminder information is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, so we don't collect your name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Children

Giftara is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your information

Everything you keep in Giftara is on your phone: clear your conversations in Settings, delete people or gifts in the app, or uninstall Giftara to remove it. The server's chat counts and Premium status are tied only to a random install ID; if you'd like them deleted, email us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
